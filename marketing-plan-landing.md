   Design direction
   I would keep this page very clean and premium rather than making it look like a SaaS pricing template. Use the same light design language, typography, rounded cards, spacing and colors already established across the ApnaClinic website. Avoid black/dark backgrounds.
   The Basic card can stay simple with a subtle border, while the Pro card gets stronger brand-color accents and a small “Recommended” badge. Use real Pakistani doctor/clinic visuals where appropriate, plus small dashboard/profile mockups rather than generic medical imagery.
   One important wording improvement: instead of calling it simply “Marketing Page” in the website navigation, I would use “Plans for Doctors”, “Doctor Plans”, or “Grow Your Practice”. It communicates the purpose much more clearly.
